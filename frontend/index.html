11 — Common questions

Before you book a call.

If you have a question that isn't here, just ping me — usually 24-hour reply.

How long does a typical website project take? +

Most marketing sites and landing pages ship in 2–3 weeks. SaaS dashboards or e-commerce builds typically run 4–8 weeks depending on scope, integrations, and content readiness. We agree on milestones up-front so the timeline is never a mystery.

Do you offer monthly retainers or only fixed-scope projects? +

Three ways, actually. Website subscriptions start at ₹429/month (or annually) and cancel with 30 days' notice. SEO and performance-marketing retainers run month to month and cancel with 14 days' notice. Fixed-scope builds are billed 50/50 — deposit on kickoff, balance on launch. Pay-per-lead is available for select industries.

Who owns the code and assets after delivery? +

You do. Full intellectual property transfers to the client once final payment clears. You get the GitHub repo, deploy credentials, design source files, and documentation. I keep no lock-in switches.

Can you guarantee #1 Google rankings or specific ROAS? +

No — and you should be sceptical of anyone who does. What I commit to: best-in-class execution, transparent dashboards, and weekly metric reviews. Outcomes depend on niche, offer quality, and budget — variables we tune together.

Do you work with international clients? +

Yes. I'm based in India and currently serve clients across the US, UK, UAE, and Singapore. Communication is async-first (Loom + Slack/email), with weekly sync calls scheduled to your timezone.

What payment methods do you accept? +

India: UPI, NEFT/RTGS, GST-compliant invoices. International: Stripe, Wise, or bank wire (USD/GBP/AED/SGD). Crypto on request. All invoicing is GST-registered under MSME.

What if I'm not happy with the work? +

Every milestone has acceptance criteria and a built-in revision round. If something genuinely misses the mark, we fix it. Refund and replacement terms are documented in the Refund Policy.

Do you handle hosting & ongoing maintenance? +

Yes. Deploys go to Vercel / Cloudflare / your AWS account. Post-launch retainers include uptime monitoring, security patches, dependency updates, and a monthly performance audit. No surprise bills.
